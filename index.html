How estimates work

The first version estimates home/away scoring rates from recent completed matches available in the selected competition window, then uses a simple Poisson goal model to calculate home win, draw, away win, over 2.5 goals and both-teams-to-score probabilities. Small samples are marked as limited evidence. These are statistical estimates, not AI certainty, bookmaker odds or guarantees.
